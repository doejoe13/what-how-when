How many ounces in a cup?

Quick answer: 8 fluid ounces (US) = 1 cup = 236.6 ml. In the UK, 1 imperial cup = 10 imperial fl oz = 284 ml.

Common conversions

  • 1 US cup = 8 fl oz = 16 tablespoons = 48 teaspoons = 236.6 ml
  • 1 US cup of water weighs 8.35 oz by weight (not the same as fl oz)
  • 1 UK/imperial cup = 10 imperial fl oz = 284 ml
  • 1 metric cup (AU/NZ) = 250 ml
  • 1 US pint = 2 cups = 16 fl oz
  • 1 US quart = 4 cups = 32 fl oz
  • 1 US gallon = 16 cups = 128 fl oz

Fluid oz vs. weight oz

A fluid ounce measures volume; an ounce (oz) measures weight. They're not interchangeable. 1 US cup of flour weighs about 4.5 oz, not 8.
