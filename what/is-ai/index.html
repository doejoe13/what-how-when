What is AI?

Quick answer: AI (artificial intelligence) is software that performs tasks that normally require human intelligence — like understanding language, recognizing images, or making decisions.

Types of AI

  • Narrow AI — specialized at one task (chatbots, image recognition, recommendations). This is everything in use today.
  • General AI (AGI) — hypothetical, human-level reasoning across any task. Not yet achieved.
  • Superintelligence — theoretical, beyond human capability. Speculative.

How modern AI works

Most current AI uses machine learning: models learn patterns from large datasets rather than being explicitly programmed. Large language models (LLMs) like GPT are trained on massive text corpora to predict the next word, which turns out to be enough to hold conversations, write code, and answer questions.

FAQ

Is AI conscious?

No. Current AI has no awareness, feelings, or understanding — it produces statistically likely outputs.

Will AI take my job?

It changes jobs more than it eliminates them. Tasks that are repetitive and text- or image-based are most affected.
