Who is the richest person in the world?

Quick answer: The title changes month to month and depends on stock prices that move daily. The only reliable sources are live rankings.

Live rankings

  • Forbes Real-Time Billionaires — forbes.com/real-time-billionaires
  • Bloomberg Billionaires Index — bloomberg.com/billionaires

Recent top names

In the last few years, the top spot has alternated between Elon Musk (Tesla, SpaceX), Bernard Arnault (LVMH), and Jeff Bezos (Amazon). It depends entirely on the day you check.
