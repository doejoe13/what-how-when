How long to boil eggs?

Quick answer: 4 min for soft-boiled, 6 min for medium, 8–10 min for hard-boiled — after the water reaches a rolling boil.

Times by doneness

  • 3 minutes — very runny yolk, barely set white
  • 4 minutes — soft-boiled: runny yolk, set white
  • 6 minutes — medium: jammy, slightly runny yolk
  • 8 minutes — hard-boiled: firm but slightly creamy yolk
  • 10 minutes — fully hard-boiled
  • 12+ minutes — chalky yolk, green ring appears

Method

  1. Lower eggs gently into already-boiling water.
  2. Start the timer as soon as they hit the water.
  3. When done, transfer to ice water for 2 minutes — this stops cooking and makes them easier to peel.
