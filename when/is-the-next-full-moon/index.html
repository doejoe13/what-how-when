How this is calculated

The Moon's cycle averages 29.53 days. A known reference full moon (2000-01-06 18:14 UTC) is used as a base, and the interval is extended forward to the next occurrence.

For precision (minutes-level), use an ephemeris source — dates here can be off by a few hours depending on your time zone.
