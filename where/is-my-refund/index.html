Where's my refund?

Quick answer: Use your country's official tax agency refund tracker. Don't trust third-party sites — they often charge for free information.

Official trackers

  • United States — irs.gov/wheres-my-refund
  • United Kingdom — gov.uk/claim-tax-refund
  • Canada — canada.ca → Refunds
  • Australia — ato.gov.au → Refunds
  • Germany — bzst.de

Typical wait times

  • US e-file with direct deposit: 21 days or less
  • US paper return: 6–8 weeks
  • UK: usually 5 working days after the claim is approved
  • Canada: 2 weeks e-file, 8 weeks paper
