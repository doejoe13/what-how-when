How to screenshot on Mac

Quick answer: Press ⌘ + ⇧ + 3 for the whole screen, or ⌘ + ⇧ + 4 to select an area.

All shortcuts

  • ⌘ ⇧ 3 — full screen → saved to Desktop
  • ⌘ ⇧ 4 — drag to select an area
  • ⌘ ⇧ 4 then Space — click a window to capture just it
  • ⌘ ⇧ 5 — screenshot toolbar: options for selection, window, screen recording
  • ⌘ ⇧ 6 — Touch Bar screenshot (on supported MacBooks)
  • Add ⌃ (Control) to any of the above to copy to clipboard instead of saving a file

Where do screenshots go?

By default to your Desktop, named "Screenshot [date] at [time].png". You can change the folder in the ⌘⇧5 toolbar → Options.
