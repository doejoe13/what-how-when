How to delete a Facebook account

Quick answer: Go to facebook.com/settings → Your Facebook Information → Deactivation and Deletion, choose Delete account, and confirm.

Permanent deletion

  1. On facebook.com: click your profile picture → Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Left sidebar → Your Facebook Information → Deactivation and Deletion.
  3. Select Delete account → Continue to account deletion.
  4. Enter your password and confirm.

Facebook waits 30 days before permanently removing the account. Log in during that window to cancel.

Temporary deactivation

  1. Same menu path as above, but choose Deactivate account.
  2. Pick a reason, confirm.

Your profile disappears but the data stays. Log back in to reactivate.

Before you delete

  • Download your data: Settings → Your Facebook Information → Download your information.
  • Check which apps and sites use "Log in with Facebook".
  • Save photos and messages you want to keep.
