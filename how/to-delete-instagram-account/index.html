How to delete an Instagram account

Quick answer: Go to instagram.com/accounts/remove/request/permanent/, log in, pick a reason, and confirm. Instagram gives you 30 days to change your mind.

Permanent deletion

  1. Open a browser (not the app) and go to Instagram's deletion page.
  2. Log in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Choose a reason from the dropdown.
  4. Enter your password and click Permanently delete my account.

Your account and data are removed after 30 days. Log in during that window to cancel.

Temporary disable

  1. Open the Instagram app → Profile → Menu → Settings.
  2. Account → Delete account → Deactivate account.
  3. Choose why, enter password, confirm.

You can reactivate anytime by logging back in.

Before you delete

  • Download your data: Settings → Your activity → Download your information.
  • Note any accounts you logged in with Instagram.
  • Save photos and DMs you want to keep.
