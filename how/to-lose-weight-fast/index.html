How to lose weight fast

Quick answer: Eat ~500 kcal less than you burn per day, prioritize protein and fiber, walk 8–10k steps, sleep 7–8 hours, and drink water before meals. That's ~0.5 kg (1 lb) per week — the fastest safe rate.

What actually works

  • Calorie deficit — 300–500 kcal/day below maintenance.
  • Protein — 1.6–2.2 g per kg body weight preserves muscle.
  • Fiber — 25–40 g/day keeps you full.
  • Movement — 8–10k steps plus 2–3 strength sessions a week.
  • Sleep — 7–8 hours; short sleep raises hunger hormones.
  • Water — 500 ml before meals reduces intake.

What to avoid

  • Crash diets under 1,200 kcal without medical supervision.
  • "Detox" teas and fat-burner pills.
  • Cutting entire food groups you enjoy — it doesn't last.

FAQ

How fast can I safely lose weight?

0.5–1 kg (1–2 lb) per week. Faster usually means muscle and water loss.

Do I need to count calories?

Not forever, but tracking for 2–3 weeks teaches portion awareness fast.
